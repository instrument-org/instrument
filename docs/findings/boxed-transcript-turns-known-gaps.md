# Boxed transcript turns: known gaps

**Status:** open, checked 2026-11-27. Each gap below is small and was left when far turns became boxes, or when the turns not loaded yet got room of their own ([transcript-virtualization.md](../plans/transcript-virtualization.md), [far-rows.ts](../../apps/studio/src/client/components/ui/scroll-owner/far-rows.ts), `OlderTurns` in [transcript.tsx](../../apps/studio/src/client/components/transcript/transcript.tsx)); none loses words, and the first is the only one that moves the reader's place in the checks that exist.

- **A turn a link reached stays drawn.** A quote chip or `#heading` link into a boxed turn draws it through `keepDrawn`, and nothing releases it until the next such link, so one far turn can stay drawn for the life of the transcript.
- **A heading link scrolls on its own.** It lands with one smooth scroll to a fixed position rather than through `ScrollOwner`. Unchecked: whether boxes above drawing during that scroll push the heading off the spot.
- **A link inside a table or the composer searches only that container.** Older than boxing; boxing only makes the miss likelier.
- **Streaming during a zoom skews box heights.** A box with no height measured at the new width scales by the ratio the drawn rows changed by in the same resize, and a drawn row growing from streamed text in that resize counts as zoom.
- **A wrap choice made while a code block's first line streams is lost if its turn then boxes.**
- **Two code blocks in one turn with the same language and first line share their wrap and expand state** in `kept-open.ts`, since that is what keys them.
- **The first turn held loses its date line when a page lands above it.** `chatSeparators` reads the first message it is given as the chat's first, so the first turn held shows the day, time and model above its message, and a page arriving above takes the line away. When the browser anchors on that turn's row, what is under it moves up by the line's height (38 px at 1x). Older than the room for turns not loaded; the room's browser tests send their uneven chat's turns hours apart, so every turn keeps a line, to keep this out of what they check.
- **A last page of a turn or two can end a drag to the top just short of the first turn.** When the room left is shorter than the view, turns show under it at the very top, and the owner keeps the one in view where it was as the last page lands, so the view ends that page's misestimate from the top (or, when the page is shorter than its room, the turns in view move up by the difference, since the position cannot go under 0).
- **The room for turns not loaded is estimated once per page.** It is taken when the count changes, from the turns held as first laid out: pictures and cards that size themselves later, and a zoom or a narrower window, leave it as it was until the next page. On `long-work-chat` the column at opening is 158,076 px against 169,602 with every turn in, 7% short, since its last 20 turns run shorter than the chat's average; each page then takes the room again from more turns, so wheeling to the top changes the column's length 25 times, by 12,618 px in all and at most 10,813 at once.
