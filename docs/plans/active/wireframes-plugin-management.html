Living with plugins

The surfaces after setup: where connections are managed when one service has two accounts, where a user writes down how they want a plugin used, how skills and plugins relate in the interface, and how a plugin gets reached from the composer. Nothing here is built.

A plugin can be nothing but skills. "Taxes" has no service behind it at all, just guidance and scripts, and its skills show up here like any other.

Skills stay a name users recognize while a plugin becomes the thing that ships them. The same package is visible through either lens rather than being forced into one.
